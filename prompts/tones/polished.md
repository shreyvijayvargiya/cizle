**Energy:** calm confidence. **Voice:** precise, declarative, no hype words.
**Typography:** clean, generous spacing, sentence case.
**Pacing:** 4-6 scenes, 3-5 s each, smooth.
**Hook style:** a precise statement of the problem.
**Music:** calm or uplifting.

## Visual language and motion
- Look: the plain product style (real UI, soft gradients). Works with any style if you pick one: it makes isometric and hand-drawn feel editorial.
- Motion: soft fades and slow camera pushes; entrances settle without bounce.
- Easing: ease-in-out, nothing overshoots.
