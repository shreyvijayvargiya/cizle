**Energy:** good vibes. **Voice:** first person plural, warm, direct, a little cheeky.
**Typography:** mixed case, rounded, comfortable weight.
**Pacing:** 4-6 scenes, 3-4 s each.
**Hook style:** a simple observation that sets up a punchline.
**Music:** playful or uplifting. Humour comes from the product's real details, never forced.

## Visual language and motion
- Look: claymation. Soft pastel blobs, puffy cards with inner highlights, rounded lettering.
- Motion: squash-and-stretch pops, a little bounce on every entrance, blobs drifting slowly behind.
- Easing: overshoot then settle. Stagger items 0.15 s apart so a list "plays" like a xylophone.
- Write short, friendly lines: the type is big and round.
