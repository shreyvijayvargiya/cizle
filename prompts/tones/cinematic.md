**Energy:** movie trailer. **Voice:** short, weighty lines with pauses.
**Typography:** huge, bold, few words per scene.
**Pacing:** builds slowly then hits hard; 2-3 s scenes, a held reveal.
**Hook style:** a dramatic one-liner about the status quo.
**Music:** cinematic. Claims must still be true.

## Visual language and motion
- Look: glassmorphism. Dark gradient, huge soft colour orbs, frosted cards with a thin bright edge.
- Motion: slow blurred fade-ups into focus; the orbs drift like light leaks; the reveal lands after a beat of near-darkness.
- Easing: long, smooth decelerations. Cut on the beat of the music.
- One short line per card: the glow is the drama, not the words.
