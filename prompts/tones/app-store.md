**Energy:** Apple keynote feature card. **Voice:** benefit-first, one idea per line.
**Typography:** crisp, title case captions.
**Pacing:** even rhythm, 3-4 s per scene, one feature per card.
**Hook style:** the single best benefit, stated plainly.
**Music:** uplifting or techy.

## Visual language and motion
- Look: isometric. A light grid floor, extruded white slabs with a coloured side, small floating cubes in brand colours.
- Motion: cards slide in along the 30-degree axis and click into place; cubes bob gently.
- Easing: crisp, slightly mechanical. Each card enters 0.15 s after the one before.
- Feature name on the top face, benefit beneath it: never more than two lines.
