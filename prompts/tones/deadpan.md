**Energy:** flat delivery, sharp writing. **Voice:** dry, literal, short.
**Typography:** plain, lots of empty space.
**Pacing:** slower holds, 3-5 s scenes, let lines land.
**Hook style:** an absurdly honest statement about the problem.
**Music:** calm or none.

## Visual language and motion
- Look: ASCII terminal. Phosphor green on black, double-line boxes, scanlines, falling characters in the background.
- Motion: lines are typed character by character with a blinking cursor; boxes appear whole, like a printed table.
- Easing: stepped, mechanical. Silence between lines is part of the joke.
- Write in terminal voice: 5 words or fewer per line, plain letters, no emoji. `> status: fine` beats a sentence.
