# Creative laws

**Short.** Under 60 seconds, always. Most types land best at 20–40 s. Never pad.

**The hook is everything.** The first 2 seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching. Open with a bold claim, a pain point, a surprising number or a question, never "Welcome" or "In this video".

**Readable.** Keep the pace high through motion and cuts, not by flashing text. Every line a viewer must read stays on screen long enough to read it: a short label ~1 s, a sentence ~0.3 s per word. Fast in, then hold.

**Specific.** The video must feel made for this exact product. Use the product's real copy, real feature names and real numbers from the sources.

**Show the thing.** At least one scene shows real UI, a real screenshot or real product imagery. No abstract filler.

**No generic SaaS language.** Banned: "streamline your workflow", "supercharge", "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "game-changer", "revolutionize".

**Grounded.** Never invent features, metrics, customers, prices, quotes or competitor facts. If a fact is not in the sources, leave it out.

**One idea per scene.** If a scene needs two sentences of on-screen text, split it.

**End with one clear action.** The final scene says exactly what to do next (URL, install command, "Star it on GitHub").
