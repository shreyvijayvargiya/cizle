# Google display banner set

**Goal:** a set of static Google Ads display banners for any brand: an online shop, a local business, an app, a creator or a SaaS. These are images, not a video. Write the banner copy; cizle draws every size in the chosen style.

## Format
Responsive display images are usually 1.91:1 (1200 by 628) and square (1200 by 1200). The classic fixed sizes are 300x250, 336x280, 728x90, 300x600, 160x600 and 320x50. Sizes and file limits change, so check the current Google Ads specs before you upload. Keep each banner to one message.

## Banner copy (write one to three variants)
1. `headline`: the benefit in 8 words or fewer.
2. `subhead` (optional): one supporting line. It is dropped in the thin sizes.
3. `cta`: Shop now, Book today, Try it free, Learn more.
4. `offer` (optional): only if real.

Each variant takes a different angle (the benefit, the problem, the offer) and shares one brand and one call to action. Every size is drawn for every variant.

## By brand
- **Online shop / e-commerce:** the product in hand or in use, the one benefit, the offer only if the brand states it (price, bundle, free shipping). Close on "Shop now" and the shop URL.
- **Beauty and fashion:** the texture, fit or finish up close; the situation it is worn or used in. No before/after claims. Close on "Shop the look" or "Try it".
- **Food and drink:** the product being made, poured or eaten in the first second; one taste word; the place or the delivery option. Close on "Order now".
- **Health and fitness:** the routine, the equipment or the class, never a promised body or medical result. Close on "Start free" or "Book a class".
- **Local services:** the problem fixed, the area served and a phone or booking action. Close on "Book today" or "Call now".
- **Apps and games:** the core loop on a real screen recording or screenshot, one reaction beat. Close on "Get the app".
- **SaaS and dev tools:** the painful task, the product doing it on a real screen, one measurable result only if real. Close on "Try it free".
- **Creators and agencies:** the channel, course or service in one line, one proof point that is real (subscriber count, a client name only if allowed). Close on "Subscribe", "Join" or "Book a call".

## Rules
- Headline at most 8 words, subhead at most 14, call to action at most 4 words, offer at most 4 words.
- Only a real offer the brand stated. No invented prices, discounts, ratings, reviews or awards.
- Name the brand. If you have a product photo URL, pass it as `image` (https only).
- Follow the ads laws: one idea, one offer, one action, true claims.
- Choose the style from the brand: clean for most, hand-drawn for friendly services, clay for playful consumer brands, glass for tech, ASCII for developer tools.
