# Use-case spotlight

**Goal:** a viewer in one specific role thinks "this is for me". Make one cut per audience (agencies, recruiters, indie hackers) and send each where that audience hangs out.

## Structure and motion
1. **Call-out (2-3 s)** `title`: "For <audience>" with the sharpest pain of that role as the subtitle.
2. **Their day today (4 s)** `bullets`: three things that waste their time, in their words.
3. **Their day with the product (8-10 s)** two `screenshot` scenes, one capability each, captioned with the outcome for this role.
4. **The result (3 s, only if real)** `stats`: a number from the sources that matters to this persona.
5. **Outro (3 s)** `outro`: a CTA phrased for the audience ("Start your agency pipeline").

## In each style
- **Clean:** the persona name is the hero; screenshots carry the story.
- **Clay:** a friendly badge with the role pops in first, then the benefits.
- **Glass:** the role chip glows, cards show the before and after.
- **Isometric:** the workflow as a small factory line of blocks.
- **Hand-drawn:** a sketched day-in-the-life with a tick next to each solved annoyance.
- **ASCII:** `whoami` prints the role, then `run workflow`.

## Rules
- The audience comes from the brief's `audience` and `pains`. Do not invent a persona the sources do not describe.
- Use the words the persona would use, not marketing words.
- One persona per video. If the product serves several, pick the one the sources talk about most.
