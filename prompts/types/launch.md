# Launch teaser

**Goal:** hype. Make people want to click on launch day (Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn, a GitHub release).

## Structure
1. **Tension (2–3 s)** — a short line about the problem or the status quo.
2. **Build (2–3 scenes, 2 s each)** — very short lines, one or two words of big type, quick cuts.
3. **Reveal (3 s)** — product name + one-liner over the best screenshot.
4. **One proof beat (2 s, optional)** — a real number.
5. **Outro (3 s)** — "Live now" / "Out today" + URL.

## Rules
- Fewer words than any other type. Big, bold typography.
- Pace: fast. Music mood: cinematic or energetic.
- If narration is on, it is sparse and dramatic: short sentences with pauses.
