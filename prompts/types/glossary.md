# Glossary explainer

**Goal:** educational short that makes the viewer smarter about the space, and positions the product as the expert.

## Structure
1. **Hook (2–3 s)** — "3 terms every <audience> should know" or a confusing term as a question.
2. **3–4 terms** — each a `glossary` scene: the term big, a plain-English definition (≤ 18 words), and an optional one-line example.
3. **Tie-in (3–4 s)** — screenshot of the product handling one of the terms.
4. **Outro (3 s)** — CTA.

## Rules
- Take terms from the brief's `glossary` (docs, blog posts, llms.txt). Define them in plain language, no jargon inside definitions.
- Each definition must be accurate for the general concept, not marketing copy.
- Order from most familiar to most advanced.
