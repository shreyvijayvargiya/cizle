# Product showcase

**Goal:** someone who has never heard of the product understands what it is, who it is for and why it is better, in one watch.

## Structure
1. **Hook (2–3 s)** — the sharpest pain point or the boldest true claim from the brief.
2. **Reveal (3–4 s)** — product name + one-liner over the best hero screenshot.
3. **Three moments (4–6 s each)** — one capability per scene, each on its own real screenshot with a 2–4 word on-screen caption.
4. **Proof (3 s, only if the brief has proof)** — stars, users, a metric or a short quote.
5. **Outro (3 s)** — name, CTA, URL.

## Rules
- Prefer screenshots with clear UI over marketing hero art.
- Captions name the outcome ("Invoices in one click"), not the feature label ("Invoice module").
- Camera moves: zoom_in on single elements, pan_down on long pages.
