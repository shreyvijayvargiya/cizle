# Ads laws

These apply when the type is an ad format. They add to the creative laws above.

**Hook in two seconds.** The first frame must already be the point: the problem, the result, the offer or a bold claim. No logo-first intros, no "Hi, I'm".

**One ad, one idea, one offer, one call to action.** A single audience, a single promise, a single action at the end.

**Sound off.** Most ads play muted. Every message must read on screen in large type; narration only repeats what the screen says.

**Stay out of the platform's UI.** Keep key text and the product away from the top, bottom and sides where the app draws its buttons and captions. Each ad type lists its safe margins.

**Show the product being used.** The item, the app screen or the service result must be visible within the first third of the ad.

**Only true claims.** Never invent prices, discounts, review counts, star ratings, awards, "as seen on", before/after results, customer quotes or testimonials, or health, income and savings claims. Use only what the brand supplied. If the brief names no offer, do not make one up: lead with the benefit.

**Follow the ad policies.** No misleading urgency ("only 2 left") unless the brand says so, no personal-attribute callouts ("Are you overweight?"), no before/after body claims, no fake interface elements (a play button, a notification) that do nothing.

**Say who it is for.** Name the audience or the situation on screen in the first seconds, so the right viewer stops.

**Close with the action.** The last frame shows the brand name, the offer if there is one, and one action: "Shop now", "Try it free", "Book today", "Get the app", "Learn more". Hold it long enough to read.

**Verify the specs.** The sizes, lengths and safe zones in each ad type are a good starting point and they change. Check the platform's current ad specs before you run the ad.
