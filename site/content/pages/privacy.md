# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

## What we collect

- **Account details.** When you sign in we receive your email address and, with Google sign-in, your name and profile picture, through Firebase Authentication.
- **Subscription status.** Whether you have Premium and until when, so we can unlock skills for you.
- **Messages.** If you email us, we keep the conversation to reply to you.
- **Usage data.** Basic, privacy-friendly page analytics may be used to see which skills are popular. We do not sell data.

## What we do not collect

We do not see your card details: payments are handled by Dodo Payments. The videos you create with cizle are rendered on your own machine; we never receive them or the product links you use.

## Who processes data

- **Google Firebase** for sign-in and to store your subscription status.
- **Dodo Payments** for payments, taxes and invoices, under their own privacy policy.
- **Our hosting provider** for serving the website.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing hello@cizle.dev. You can sign out and clear local storage at any time; the site stores only your theme choice and your sign-in session in your browser.
