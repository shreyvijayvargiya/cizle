# Refund policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

If cizle Premium is not what you hoped, email **hello@cizle.dev** within **7 days of your first payment** from the address you paid with, and we will refund that payment in full. No questions.

- Renewal payments are not refunded after the fact; cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method by Dodo Payments and can take a few business days to appear.
- If you were charged twice or you paid and Premium did not unlock, contact us and we will put it right straight away.
