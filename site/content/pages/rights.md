# Credits, licences and removal

cizle is a library of prompts and skills made by other people. This page says how we credit them, what we show in full, and how to have your work changed or removed. We try to do this properly, and we fix mistakes fast.

## What we collect and from where

- **Prompts** that creators published in public posts on X, and in public GitHub repositories and websites that list prompts.
- **Skills** (`SKILL.md` files) from public GitHub repositories.
- Only what is public. We do not collect anything from private accounts, paywalled pages or messages.

## How we credit

- Every prompt shows its creator, links to the original post or page, and the collection and date it was collected.
- The [creators page](/creators) lists every creator with a link to their own profile.
- Videos and pictures are shown from the place they are published (X, the creator's site or repository). We do not copy them to our servers.

## What is shown in full, and what is not

- Prompts that creators shared publicly on X are shown in full, with credit and a link back.
- Content from a repository or site is shown in full **only when it has an open licence** (for example MIT, Apache-2.0, CC0 or CC-BY). If there is no licence, or we cannot tell, we show a short preview and a link, and the full text stays with its creator. Those pages say "Preview only".
- Each skill and prompt lists its licence when we know it. If you hold the rights and want your work shown in full, tell us and we will switch it on.

## Have your work changed or removed

Email [hello@cizle.dev](mailto:hello@cizle.dev) with your X or GitHub handle (or the link to the prompt or skill) and what you want: removed, changed, or shown in full. We usually act within one working day. A removal takes your entries off the library, the creators page and the sitemap; you do not need to give a reason.

You can also use the link at the bottom of every prompt, "Are you the creator and want this changed or removed?".

## Things that are not ours

- The prompts, skills, pictures and videos belong to their creators. Listing something here does not give anyone a right to reuse it beyond what its creator or licence allows.
- Check the licence at the source before you reuse a skill commercially.
- We do not claim any model or tool made a prompt's output unless the creator said so.

## Corrections

If a credit, a link or a licence on a page is wrong, email us the page address and the correction.
