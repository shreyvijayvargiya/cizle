Most SaaS founders make one video, the launch video, and stop. But different questions need different videos. Here are the ten that earn their place, with the structure that makes each one work.

## 1. Product showcase

Show the product doing its job in 20 to 30 seconds. Structure: one-line promise, three real screens, one outcome, call to action. Use the [Product showcase](/skills/showcase) skill.

## 2. Feature tour

One video per feature beats one video for everything. Show the problem, the feature, the result. See [Feature spotlight](/skills/features).

## 3. How it works

Three to five numbered steps, one idea per step. Perfect for the top of your docs and your onboarding emails. See [How it works](/skills/how-it-works).

## 4. Quickstart

Install, configure, first result. Developers watch this one with their terminal open, so keep every command readable. See [Quickstart](/skills/quickstart).

## 5. FAQ video

Answer the five objections buyers raise on calls. Each answer is a card, each card is 4 to 5 seconds. See [Product FAQ](/skills/faq).

## 6. What's new

A release-notes video that people actually watch: three changes, one screen each. See [What's new](/skills/whats-new).

## 7. Comparison

Be honest about where you fit against the alternative. Two columns, a handful of criteria, no unsupported claims. See [Comparison](/skills/comparison).

## 8. Customer proof

A real quote, a real number, a real logo, with permission. See [Customer proof](/skills/customer-proof).

## 9. Founder story

Why you built it, in 30 seconds. People buy from people. See [Founder story](/skills/founder-story).

## 10. Pricing explained

Pricing pages are where deals go to die. A short video that explains the plans removes the guesswork. See [Pricing explained](/skills/pricing-explained).
