Each ad platform rewards a different rhythm. The same product needs a different video for a Reels placement, a TikTok feed and a YouTube bumper. This guide covers the formats we build skills for and the structure behind each.

## Meta Reels and Stories: 15 seconds, vertical

Vertical 9:16, about 15 seconds. The first two seconds must state the sharpest pain point or the boldest true claim. Then show the product in action, one offer, one action. See the [Meta Reels hook ad](/skills/meta-reels-hook) skill.

## Meta feed: offer and problem-solution

In the feed people scroll quickly, so lead with the offer or the problem in text. The [Meta feed offer](/skills/meta-feed-offer) and [problem-solution](/skills/meta-problem-solution) skills give you both structures.

## TikTok: native, not polished

TikTok ads work when they look like TikToks: a spoken-style hook, fast cuts, captions burned in. We cover three patterns: a [UGC-style hook](/skills/tiktok-ugc-hook), an [offer drop](/skills/tiktok-offer-drop) and a [demo listicle](/skills/tiktok-demo-listicle).

## Google and YouTube: from six seconds to a minute

- **Bumper, 6 seconds.** One message only: the [Google bumper](/skills/google-bumper-6s) skill.
- **In-stream, 15 seconds.** A hook before the skip button: [Google in-stream](/skills/google-instream-15s).
- **Shorts, vertical.** Treat it like a short organic video: [Google Shorts ad](/skills/google-shorts-ad).
- **Display banners.** Static images in the standard sizes: [Google display banners](/skills/google-display-banners).

## What every format has in common

1. **A hook in the first two seconds.** If it is not clear by then, it is skipped.
2. **One idea.** One audience, one promise, one action.
3. **Proof, not adjectives.** A number, a screen or a quote from your own pages.
4. **A clear last frame.** The call to action stays long enough to read.
