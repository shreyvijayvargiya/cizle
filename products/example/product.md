## Positioning
Example CRM replaces the shared spreadsheet agencies use to track deals. Every deal lives on one pipeline board that the whole team can see and update.

## Who it's for
Small agencies with 2–15 people.

## Features
- **Pipeline board** — drag a deal between stages and everyone sees it at once. *(site:/features)*
- **Email sync** — conversations attach to the right deal automatically. *(github:README)*

## Pricing
- **Free** — up to 3 users
- **Team** — $12 per user per month

## Tone
Plain, practical, no hype.

## Hooks
- Your pipeline lives in a spreadsheet. That is the problem.
