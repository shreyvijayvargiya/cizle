# Prompt 01: AI Garage Weekly Newsletter Promo

By @tonbistudio · prompts
Language: en · Domains: product, education, science · Source: M03

Source page: https://github.com/tonbistudio/hyperframes-prompts/blob/HEAD/prompts/01-newsletter-promo.md

```
Create a 25-second 16:9 HyperFrames promotional video for AI Garage Weekly.

Source:
https://www.onchainaigarage.com

Goal:
Drive newsletter signups by showing that AI Garage Weekly is a fast, fun, useful Friday dispatch for AI tinkerers, builders, and curious technical people.

Audience:
AI builders, indie hackers, onchain AI people, local AI experimenters, and people who follow Onchain AI Garage.

Core message:
A newsletter for AI tinkerers. Latest AI news, interesting research finds, current ML experiments, and unique project ideas. Free every Friday. 5 minute read. From the creator of Onchain AI Garage. 10K+ subscribers and 300K+ views.

Format:
16:9 landscape, 1920x1080.
Suitable for YouTube, X, LinkedIn, and newsletter landing page embeds.
No talking head.
The video should work silently with captions.
Optional upbeat music and light SFX.

Visual identity:
Match the actual AI Garage Weekly site closely.

Use:
- bright cyan / sky-blue hero background
- cream editorial paper sections
- red comic-style AI GARAGE WEEKLY headline with black outline
- thick black borders
- offset shadows
- monospace uppercase labels
- hand-drawn doodle robots, stars, coins, squiggles, and paper-plane marks
- printed newsletter issue cards
- playful comic-zine / garage-lab personality

Avoid:
- corporate SaaS polish
- generic AI gradients
- holograms
- glassmorphism
- stock footage
- excessive shader effects

Scene structure:

Scene 1: 0-4 seconds
Hook.
Open on the cyan hero background.
Doodles pop in around the frame: robot, star, coin, squiggle, paper plane.
Stamp in the label:
A NEWSLETTER FOR AI TINKERERS
Then slam in the huge red headline:
AI GARAGE WEEKLY

Caption:
"A newsletter for AI tinkerers."

Motion:
- sticker-stamp reveal
- bouncy title overshoot
- tiny doodle loops
- comic-zine wobble

Scene 2: 4-8 seconds
Credibility.
Animate proof badges from the landing page:
FROM THE CREATOR OF ONCHAIN AI GARAGE
10K+ SUBSCRIBERS
300K+ VIEWS

Caption:
"From the creator of Onchain AI Garage."

Motion:
- badges pop in like printed labels
- light paper texture
- red underline scribbles
- quick camera push

Scene 3: 8-14 seconds
What you get.
Show four kinetic paper cards:
1. AI news
2. Research finds
3. ML experiments
4. Project ideas

Caption:
"News, research, experiments, and project ideas."

Motion:
- paper cards slide and shuffle
- card edges lift like physical zines
- doodle arrows connect the cards
- one comic-panel transition between beats

Scene 4: 14-19 seconds
Latest issues.
Show recent issue cards fanning into frame like printed zines.
Use real issue topics from the site if visible, such as:
- Hermes Agent Masterclass
- Gemma MTP
- VLM Reinforcement Learning
- Local AI
- Brain Upload
- Codex for Work
- Multi-Agent Systems
- Hermes Dashboard Plugins
- Kimi K2.6
- Hermes + Webhooks
- LeWorldModel
- Zasshi

Caption:
"Five-minute reads. Real experiments."

Motion:
- cards fan open
- camera glides over issue titles
- one card gets circled with a hand-drawn marker

Scene 5: 19-23 seconds
Subscribe moment.
Recreate the signup form.
Type an example email into the form.
Click the red SUBSCRIBE button.
Trigger a doodle burst.

Caption:
"Free every Friday."

Motion:
- typewriter clicks
- button press bounce
- stars and squiggles burst outward

Scene 6: 23-25 seconds
CTA.
Clean end card.

Text:
AI GARAGE WEEKLY
A newsletter for AI tinkerers
Free every Friday
onchainaigarage.com

Motion:
- logo settles
- doodles loop subtly
- final paper-card snap to frame

Technical direction:
Use website capture or deterministic HTML recreation. Preserve the actual site style. Use GSAP seekable timelines. Use only one major transition, such as a comic-panel wipe or grid-pixelate wipe. Keep text large and readable.

Deliverables:
Create DESIGN.md, SCRIPT.md, STORYBOARD.md, and the HyperFrames composition. Provide 5 still-frame checkpoints before final render:
1. hero title frame
2. credibility badges frame
3. what-you-get cards frame
4. latest issues zine spread
5. subscribe CTA frame
```
