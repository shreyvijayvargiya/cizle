# Old Friends Reunion: Painterly Fantasy Short Film

By @alamirahamirah2@gmail.com · general
Language: en · Domains: entertainment, history, music · Source: P01

Source page: https://prompts.chat

```
A cinematic fantasy short film, about 40 seconds, painterly animated style with soft volumetric lighting, warm golden tones against deep midnight blue, shallow depth of field, slow smooth camera movements, nostalgic and emotional mood, 4K, 24fps.

MAIN CHARACTER: a 30-year-old man, short dark hair, neat short beard, expressive brown eyes, wearing a simple dark jacket. Keep his face and look consistent in every shot.

SCENE 1 (0-6s): Night. The man sits alone on a quiet rooftop, looking up at a huge glowing full moon in a sky full of stars. Slow push-in on his face, moonlight on his skin, gentle wind.

SCENE 2 (6-12s): Extreme close-up of his eyes reflecting the moon. The reflection ripples like water, soft glowing particles float around him, his expression drifts into a dreamy trance.

SCENE 3 (12-20s): The moonlight swirls and fills the frame, then dissolves into an ancient world of stone ruins and torchlight. The same man, now in old traditional clothing, laughs and walks with three close friends of different looks and ages. Warm, lively, full of brotherhood.

SCENE 4 (20-28s): The group enters a vast glowing cave full of gold coins, jewels and ancient artifacts. Torchlight sparkles on the treasure. Wide establishing shot, then slow tracking over their faces full of wonder.

SCENE 5 (28-34s): The man kneels before an ornate old wooden chest with carved details. His hands rest on the lid, he slowly opens it, and a burst of golden light fills the frame.

SCENE 6 (34-40s): Out of the golden light, his three friends step out of the chest, smiling and reaching toward him. An emotional reunion, with a slow 360-degree camera circle around them as light particles drift upward. Fade out.

AUDIO: soft orchestral score with gentle piano and strings, building softly toward the final scene, ambient wind, crackling torches and echoing cave sounds.

AVOID: distorted faces, extra fingers, text or watermarks, flickering, sudden style changes, inconsistent character appearance.
```
