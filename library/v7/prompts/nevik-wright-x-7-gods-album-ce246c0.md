👑 SEVEN GOD — THE ALBUM

Genre: Spiritual Hip-Hop / Soul R&B



✨ THE STORY — FROM THE DARK INTO THE LIGHT

This isn’t just music. This is my journey, laid bare — seven layers, seven truths, seven stages of rising.

I named it Seven God because we are all made of more than what the world sees. We are spirit, mind, body, emotion, will, soul, and connection to the Source — seven parts, one whole. And every single one of mine was tested.

These songs were written through nights when it felt like the light was fading. When people I loved misunderstood me. When I poured my energy into others and got nothing back. When I questioned if anyone would ever truly see me. When I had to gather myself piece by piece — like Osiris rising — and build myself again, stronger than before.

This album holds the alchemy:

• 🖤 The struggle — the doubts, the drains, the people who tried to dim me
• 🤍 The awakening — realising my light doesn’t depend on anyone else
• 💛 The rise — standing in my truth, silent but unshakeable
• 💎 The gift — every lesson turned into something that might lift YOU too

I didn’t make this to be famous. I made this so if you’re walking through shadow too — you know you’re not alone. Your light is inside you. And it’s enough.

This music is medicine. It’s for the ones who feel different. The ones who see deeper. The ones who keep going when it would be easier to stop.

Every track is a reminder: your frequency is yours. Your path is yours. Your light doesn’t need permission to shine.
