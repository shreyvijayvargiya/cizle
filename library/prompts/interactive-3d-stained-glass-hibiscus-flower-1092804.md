# Interactive 3D stained-glass hibiscus flower

By @ivanainai · Opus 5.5 · comparison

Original post: https://x.com/ivanainai/status/2105328178051092804

Demo video: https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2105327673488617472/vid/avc1/1920x1080/Ikj8ceW5GZLcsX_b.mp4

```
Create a beautiful, interactive 3D browser artwork of a single stained-glass hibiscus flower.

Use the attached leaf as a reference for material delicacy, intricate veins, translucent colours and restrained presentation. Create an original flower composition.

The flower is the entire experience. It fills most of the viewport against a quiet, near-black charcoal background.

FLOWER

Create five broad, organically shaped petals with gently ruffled edges, subtle asymmetry and graceful curvature. Arrange them into a convincing three-dimensional hibiscus, with natural overlap and a clearly visible centre.

Each petal is made from thin, luminous stained glass. Blend ruby pink, coral, pale peach and honey amber within the glass, with delicate marbling and fine branching veins radiating from its base.

Use extremely thin antique-gold seams and a finely beveled outer edge. Preserve the delicacy of the glass. Include a slender projecting central column with tiny golden anthers, a gracefully curved stem and two small translucent green leaves.

MATERIAL AND LIGHT

The glass must have visible thickness, coloured light transmission, subtle surface irregularities and crisp reflections that change with the viewing angle.

Overlapping petals should remain readable. Balance illumination so the colours stay luminous and the veins remain visible throughout rotation. Avoid large white reflections that erase the detail.

Use soft studio lighting with a warm backlight and a restrained rim light. Avoid excessive bloom, neon colour and heavy black outlines.

MOVEMENT AND INTERACTION

Start immediately with a flattering three-quarter view. Rotate slowly and smoothly so viewers can inspect the curved petals, thin edges and reverse side.

Allow dragging to orbit, scrolling to zoom, and a small pause control. Keep the flower fully framed during automatic rotation.

DELIVERY

Build actual 3D geometry rendered in the browser. Generate the geometry and textures in code.

Deliver a working preview and a downloadable, self-contained stained-glass-flower.html with all dependencies embedded.

Run the result, inspect a full rotation, and fix missing surfaces, transparency problems, clipping or washed-out lighting before finishing. Prioritize the quality of the flower over interface decoration.
```
