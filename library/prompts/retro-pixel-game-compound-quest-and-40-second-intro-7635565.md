```
I tried using Opus 5.5 to make a retro pixel game, "Compound QUEST", for "The Evolution of Wealth".

I designed some of the difficulties on our family's path of investing and money management as levels.

In this pixel world, impulse spending is a trap to be jumped over, inflation turns into a monster that chases us. What levels up along the way is our patience stat.

Add 8-bit music, and knowledge like saving, compound interest and long-term holding gets concrete characters and levels.

After the game was finished, I used it to make this 40-second intro video.
```
