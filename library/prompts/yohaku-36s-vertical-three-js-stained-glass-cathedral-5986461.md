# Yohaku: 36s vertical three.js stained-glass cathedral film

By @AGIOyaZ · Opus 5.5 · 3d

Original post: https://x.com/AGIOyaZ/status/2103145567945986461

Demo video: https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2103145494377848833/vid/avc1/1080x1920/tu7J83z_HEbeJN_z.mp4

```
Please make a short vertical film in three.js: 1080×1920, 30 frames per second, about 36 seconds.

【Title】
Yohaku (Space to Breathe)

【The experience to convey】
A film that makes the viewer feel the sense of losing time while chasing efficiency, and the sense of life being filled with rich light the moment you gain some breathing room, without showing any person at all. The aim is for the viewer to catch their breath at the stained-glass light that finally spreads across the whole floor.

【Setting】
・The interior of a dim stone cathedral. On the front wall there is a single pointed-arch window, 15 m high and 10 m wide
・Strong light comes in from outside the window at a 45-degree angle, casting the shape of the window onto the stone floor
・The window holds stained glass with the Virgin Mary in the centre, two winged angels on either side, and a rose window at the top. The design is original, not imitating any existing work, and has a left-right symmetrical composition

【Timeline】
0–3s: The light coming through the window is still white, without colour. A soft white shape of light on the floor
3–11s: Grey cubes engraved with words such as "Busy", "Efficiency", "Urgent" and "Deadline" fly in one after another from the front of the room and fill the window. The pace of arrival keeps accelerating, and the more the window is filled, the darker the room becomes
11–14s: The window is completely blocked; darkness and silence
14–19s: A single "Busy" block comes loose from the window, falls, and vanishes into particles of light. A streak of vividly coloured light pours in through the opened hole
19–26s: Centred on the first hole, the blocks come loose in a chain reaction. With every new hole, more pillars of colourful light appear, and the hidden stained glass gradually reveals itself
26–33s: All the blocks disappear, and the camera passes through the pillars of light, rises, and looks down on the floor from directly above. Across the whole floor, the stained glass of the Virgin Mary and the angels is projected as brilliantly coloured light
33–36s: The whole screen is enveloped in dazzling light, a final line of text appears, and the film ends quietly

【Words (in Mincho typeface, appearing and fading modestly)】
・"Every day, faster."
・"More, more efficiently."
・"Before I knew it, the light had stopped coming in."
・"Let go of just one."
・"Light comes in through the empty spaces."
・"That light was richer than before."
・Finally, large: "Richness dwells in the space to breathe"

【Light and texture】
・Each pillar of light takes on the colour of that spot in the stained glass, and dust in the air sparkles
・The projection on the floor accurately reflects which holes in the window are open (where a block remains, there is shadow)
・The blocks are inorganic matte grey. The words are engraved in white
・The first half is cold and achromatic; the second half is jewel-like red, blue and gold. This colour contrast tells the story of having become richer

【Technical conditions】
・Generate the stained-glass pattern as an image, and compute the floor light, the pillars of light and the window display all from that same pattern so that they match
・Advance time precisely 1/30 of a second at a time, render out frame by frame, and produce an MP4

【Finishing】
Render and check each scene for real, and fix things before handing it over: whether the stained-glass pattern on the floor can be read as the Virgin Mary and angels, whether the words are legible, and whether any movement is abrupt.
```
