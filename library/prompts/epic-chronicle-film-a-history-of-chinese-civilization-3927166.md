# Epic chronicle film: A History of Chinese Civilization

By @dotey · Opus 5.5 · education

Original post: https://x.com/dotey/status/2103964025683927166

Demo video: https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2103961534720360448/vid/avc1/1280x720/QUw8Xd5S2z3bAu82.mp4?tag=29

```
Make an epic chronicle film, "A History of Chinese Civilization". You can write code to render frame by frame, then composite with ffmpeg.
Use the soundtrack as a clock: pentatonic modes, with instruments evolving from bone flute and bianzhong bells all the way to orchestra, BPM speeding up with the eras, and all cuts landing on the beat.
Alternate two art styles: line drawings on xuan paper and mud-gold on a dark ground; each volume gets one main colour and a set of patterns that evolve with the era (painted pottery pattern → taotie pattern → cloud-vapour pattern → scrolling-grass pattern → interlocking-branch pattern → meander pattern).
Each shot has one large calligraphy keyword that appears phrase by phrase, accompanied by a line drawing. Full-film HUD: a vermilion seal with the volume number at the top left, dynasty names in vertical text on the right, and a scroll-style timeline ruler with a year counter at the bottom.
Volume transitions are impact transitions, with a vermilion seal stamped down and a heavy drum and bell strike. First set the beat grid and the shot list, then render.
Maps are schematic only, and no real modern or contemporary figures are drawn. Verify the dates before delivery.
```
