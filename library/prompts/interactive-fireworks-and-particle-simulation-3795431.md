# Interactive Fireworks and Particle Simulation

By @FornYapayZeka · Opus 5.5 · video

Original post: https://x.com/FornYapayZeka/status/2102888241443795431

Demo video: https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2102871387123724288/vid/avc1/1280x720/3iHyPsATF7RqGbSE.mp4?tag=29

```
Produce the most impressive fireworks or luminous particle show you can make. Choose the environment and art direction yourself. Instead of merely multiplying uniform small explosions, design the rhythm, depth, environmental lighting effects, and an unforgettable finale. The show should run live; the user should be able to replay the finale.

The creative choices are yours: do not constrain the scene, art direction, camera, mechanics, technology, and interaction into predetermined, ordinary patterns. Do not settle for the first run-of-the-mill web demo that comes to mind: first briefly weigh a few ideas in your domain, pick the original one that will look most powerful in video, then turn it into a working product. Decorative imitation that makes the subject seem easier than it is will not be accepted. Cinematic and aesthetic choices should be evident in the end result.
```
