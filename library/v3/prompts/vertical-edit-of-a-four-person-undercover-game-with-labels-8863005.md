/goal I have four videos in this folder. Those four videos are four streams of four different people doing a show, like some kind of TV show or podcast, at the same time, so you'll see it's the same length.

The first thing I want you to do is to create a transcription for each and also a common transcription of the whole thing where everyone is talking, etc. Put it in any file type you find appropriate.

Then what I want you to do is to do some editing. Based on what they say, you have to guess which words they each had for that game. The game they're playing is the undercover. Here it's called Ultim Cover. There are four people playing. Among them, there are two users and two or one virus. I forgot if there were 2 vs. 1 or 3 vs. 1. You can find what it was through the transcript.

The virus is someone who has a word that is different from the others and who doesn't know his word. There's a point, maybe in the very last game they did, where the virus knows his words, but in the first ones, viruses don't know their words. The goal is to eliminate the virus, of course.

I want you to do some editing where you show four of them as a grid when it makes sense, and you show only one when one is talking specifically. It's good to have the focus on that one. Whether it's with a grid or it's focused on one person, I want you to put a card either at the top or at the bottom of the person so that we, as a viewer, know who it is, know the word they're supposed to have, and also maybe a tag saying it's the virus or whatever. Do that in a very simple design that is clean: either an orange tag with white text, or a white tag with black text, or a black tag with white text.

For now, don't do it on the whole video. Just do it for the first game they did, for the first set of words. Tell me when you're done, and we'll see later to do the rest of the video and to also have one big one for the whole video. For now, only focus on the first game. Use any tools necessary. Create any tools necessary here in a tools folder.
