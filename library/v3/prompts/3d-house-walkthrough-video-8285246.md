I turned the house I recreated with Claude Opus 5.5+Tripo+Three.js into a full room tour + interactive Vercel build.

The workflow was surprisingly simple:

Give Claude the Airbnb listing
Let it pull the reference photos and understand the space
Use Tripo for a few smaller 3D assets
Assemble and refine everything in Three.js

And that was basically enough to get a space I could actually walk through.

I have almost zero 3D skills, which still makes this feel kind of crazy.

I also tried a very similar workflow with GPT 6 Astra before, and for this specific case, Opus 5.5 was much better.

Better spatial layout, better asset placement, better overall taste, and far fewer basic mistakes to fix.

Here is the demo: https://tekapo-polaris.vercel.app/
