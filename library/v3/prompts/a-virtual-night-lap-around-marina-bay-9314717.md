When OpenAI launched Astra a few weeks ago, I used it with Blender through an MCP. My first test was to ask Astra to build Marina Bay Sands. That worked out brilliantly, but I didn't have time to go further after that. 

Given that the Singapore Grand Prix is this weekend, I revisited the project. 

This time, I thought, why stop there? So, I got Astra to recreate the entire area around Marina Bay that is part of the Singapore Grand Prix circuit. 

Then, I thought, why stop there?! So, I got Astra to find the actual track layout for the F1 night race in Singapore and create a fly-through of the track in the 3D model. 

And then, I thought, why stop there?!! So, I got Astra to use Magnific through its MCP to find realistic sound effects and sync them to the action (sound on!).  

Here's the full virtual lap of the Singapore F1 Night Race circuit for your viewing pleasure. 

The simulation is probably not a one-to-one match with the real thing. But for what its worth, its hard to find words for what the current level of frontier models can do right now, and they're just going to get more capable. 

For those of you who might be interested, a gentle reminder that the race is this weekend. Enjoy the Grand Prix!
