```
“create a 40-second kinetic typography video for [your thing]. give me a downloadable 1080p mp4 (16:9) with music and sound effects included. keep every word inside the frame.”

2. write a 5–7 line script

this is the most important part. each line is one scene.

a simple story shape that works:

* hook: a short, bold opener (“one prompt.” / “wagmi.” / “we’re live.”)
* proof: one big number (users, volume, lines of code, followers)
* what it does: 2–3 short action lines
* rhythm list: 3–4 single words that hit on the beat
* payoff: your tagline and name at the end

keep every line under 5 words. short words look better big.

3. give each scene a time

for example: 0–4s hook, 4–10s number, 10–21s features, 21–27s word list, 27–33s specs, 33–40s ending.

that tells claude where to speed up and where to slow down.

4. choose your look

pick 2 colours max, one for the background and one for the text. brand colours work best.

then choose a vibe: clean and minimal, loud and chaotic, retro, luxury.

one sentence is enough.

5. choose your music

name the genre and describe the feel.

afrobeat, amapiano, drill, lo-fi, house all work.

add:

“sync the big words to the kick and bass hits.”
```
