```
Make a 15-second looping animation, cinematic 16:9, 1920×1080. A stickman lives inside a phone battery icon and fights against power loss. The red battery-drain line is the villain. Style: minimal black ink on cream paper, monoline stick figure and interface lines, slight hand-drawn line wobble. The only color is red, reserved for power and danger: the draining battery line, warning flashes, the low-battery alert, and the impacts. Story: he's asleep on top of the fully charged battery bars and dodges the red drain line at the last moment. The battery starts draining faster, and on each cycle he survives it a different way: he jumps over the descending red level, rides the drain line like a rodeo bull as it drops, and ducks flat inside one battery bar as it sweeps past him. Finally he pries the charging bolt symbol off the center of the battery and jams the drain line with it. Power loss stops, and he takes a nap… until the low-battery alarm goes off, the screen flashes red, the battery instantly rewinds back to full, and he ends up asleep on top again, so it loops. Sound: fully synthesized in code and synced to the action (electronic ticks, warning beeps, impact hits, a metallic clank, low-battery alarm, and a digital rewind).
```
