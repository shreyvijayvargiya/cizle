```
Turn each attached portrait into a surreal photographic collage, then animate it.

Landscape 16:9, pure black background. One centered, floating face. Remove the neck, shoulders and body completely. Preserve the person’s recognizable features. Enlarge one eye, the lips and the nose. Create visible photographic cutouts, slightly shifted, tilted and overlapping.

Add a few bold, hand-drawn circles and scribbles in the Magnific color.

Use Magnific with Kling 2.5 to create a 5-second clip. Use the collage as both the first and last frame.  Keep the camera fixed and the person’s identity consistent. No talking, head turns or morphing back into a normal face.
```
