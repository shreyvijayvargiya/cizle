Here's a picture of a ship in a bottle — a tall ship in a glowing blue stormy sea inside a glass bottle on a wooden stand, with lanterns and a compass hanging off the cork. Turn it into an interactive web app and make it the best you can, a lot of people will see it.

Things I want:
- The waves should be wild and rough like the picture, real stormy water, not basic. Lots of clouds inside the bottle, but they shouldn't come out of the bottle.
- The ship should be detailed and polished, and it should actually sail around, not sit in one place. Physics on all the sails so they move in the wind.
- When I click the ship I become the captain: I'm sitting at the wheel in first person and I can steer it. Not too fast. The water must never go through the boat.
- When I'm on the ship I want to see out through the glass bottle like real glass — the room around it, reflections, the full glass effect.
- High resolution, no flickering textures, works on my phone too.
- Let me change the storm, trigger lightning, and turn sound on.
