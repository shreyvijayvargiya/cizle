```
This is my own home Wi-Fi, on my own machine. Scan it, tell me what is actually on my network, and show me where each thing sits in my apartment. Report only, change nothing.

Done means: every device listed with IP, MAC, vendor and model where you can tell, a rough map of my rooms with each device placed on it, and a separate section for anything you cannot explain.

Don't stop at a device list. Identify what each one is from vendor, hostname, open ports and service banners, and say when an identity is a guess.
Flag separately: anything streaming video or audio, anything with no vendor match or a spoofed-looking MAC, anything talking constantly to an outside IP, and anything that appeared on the network recently.
For the map: ask me to take a signal reading from each room, then place every device by room using those readings.
For each flagged device give me one line: what it is, what it is sending, how to confirm it physically, and how to cut it off.
Never change a setting, never touch passwords, never attack anything. If something needs my login, open a browser and let me do it.

End with: What you found, What is suspicious, What I need to check myself.
```
