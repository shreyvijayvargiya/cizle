Build a complete, polished browser animation called “One Naruto, Nine Worlds.”

The experience is a moving illustrated gallery: one Naruto runs sideways through nine anime worlds arranged in a 3×3 grid. As he enters each world, his anatomy, face, linework, shading and movement change to match that anime’s visual language.

1. COMPOSITION AND LAYOUT

Create a clean 3×3 gallery in this exact arrangement:

TOP ROW:
Naruto | JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure | One Piece

MIDDLE ROW:
Dragon Ball | One Punch Man | Jujutsu Kaisen

BOTTOM ROW:
Sailor Moon | Pokémon | Attack on Titan

Each panel has its own illustrated environment, a consistent frame and a small, readable title. Use a warm off-white gallery background with narrow, even gutters.

Keep Naruto relatively small: approximately one-third of a panel’s height while running normally. Leave room to appreciate the scenery, silhouettes and movement.

Only ONE Naruto is visible at a time. The other panels contain their environments while awaiting his arrival.

Keep the gallery responsive, preserve the 3×3 arrangement and use the available viewport space efficiently.

2. NARUTO’S IDENTITY

Preserve the recognizable identity across every transformation:
- Blond hair.
- Forehead protector.
- Whisker marks.
- Orange as the main clothing color.
- A recognizable Naruto-inspired outfit.

Adapt the design itself to each world. Change body proportions, facial construction, hair shapes, line weight, shadow shapes and motion.

If a reference sheet is attached, use it to guide the individual styles and composition. Build actual animation around those designs.

3. NINE DISTINCT VISUAL DIRECTIONS

NARUTO
A warm Hidden Leaf Village environment with tiled rooftops, trees and distant rocky scenery. Use classic shōnen proportions, expressive eyes and clean cel shading. Naruto runs with his torso leaning forward and his arms extended behind him.

JOJO’S BIZARRE ADVENTURE
Make the transformation unmistakable: a muscular torso, broad shoulders, strong thighs, angular facial features, sculptural shadows and confident posing. Use bold graphic colors and dramatic architectural scenery.

Keep the face clearly readable and the head proportionate to the large body. Give him a powerful stride and one brief theatrical pose, followed by a smooth return to running.

ONE PIECE
Use long, elastic arms and legs, a broad expressive grin, rounded forms and playful distortion. Place him in a colorful coastal setting with a ship, harbor details and bright sky.

Allow exaggerated limb arcs and squash-and-stretch. The movement should feel rubbery and adventurous.

DRAGON BALL
Use athletic proportions, angular blond hair, pronounced brows, bold contours and hard-edged cel shading. Create rocky terrain, distant mountains and a vivid sky.
