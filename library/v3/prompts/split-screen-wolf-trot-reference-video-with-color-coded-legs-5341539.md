PROMT (for seedance 2.5 or Omni 1.1)

Split-screen technical reference video of the same wolf filmed by two locked-off orthographic cameras at the same time.
Left panel: strict side view. Right panel: strict front view. Both panels show the exact same moment, perfectly synchronized.

MOTION: the wolf trots in place, like on a treadmill. Steady natural trot cycle with diagonal leg pairs:
BLUE and ORANGE legs swing forward together, then YELLOW and MAGENTA legs swing forward together.
The body stays in the same spot, never moves forward, never turns. Slight head bob, shoulders and hips roll with each step, tail sways gently.

LEG COLORS — the most important rule. Each leg has ONE fixed color for the whole video:
front-left = BLUE, back-left = MAGENTA PINK, front-right = YELLOW, back-right = ORANGE.
Every color is on exactly one leg. There are never two blue legs, never two pink legs, never two orange legs, never two yellow legs.
Pink stays pink, it never turns red. Orange stays orange.

LEFT PANEL (side view, wolf faces left): we see the wolf's left side.
Near legs (closer to us): BLUE front leg and MAGENTA PINK back leg.
Far legs (behind them): YELLOW front leg and ORANGE back leg.
When the legs cross, the far legs pass behind the near legs and keep their own colors.

RIGHT PANEL (front view, wolf faces us): on the viewer's right side BLUE front leg with MAGENTA PINK back leg behind it;
on the viewer's left side YELLOW front leg with ORANGE back leg behind it.

CAMERA: never moves. No zoom, no pan, no shake, no cuts. Flat solid green background and the thin black vertical divider line stay unchanged.
Fur texture on body, head and tail stays the same. Same scale, same lighting, same wolf. No text, no new objects.
