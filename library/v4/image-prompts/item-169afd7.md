```
Hinata Hyuga from Naruto,
anime character inspired Chinese ink painting portrait.

Character must remain recognizable:
dark blue long hair,
gentle expression,
pale lavender eyes,
soft and elegant personality.

Style:
Chinese freehand ink painting,
light splash ink,
焦墨飞白,
minimal blue and vermilion accents.

Theme:
a gentle oriental martial fairy standing among mountains.

Costume:
Hyuga clan elements transformed into elegant Chinese martial hanfu,
white and blue silk,
jade accessories,
flowing sleeves,
traditional embroidery.

Composition:
vertical 9:16,
huge white space,
misty mountains,
ink clouds surrounding the figure.

Pose:
soft martial arts stance,
hands forming elegant movement,
calm expression.

Atmosphere:
peaceful,
pure,
protective,
moonlight feeling.

Color:
white,
blue,
light ink gray.

No photography,
no realistic cosplay,
no modern background.
```
