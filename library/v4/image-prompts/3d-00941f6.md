Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: vertical 3D art exhibition poster.

Create a 9:16 portrait black-background poster featuring a clearly adult feminine porcelain android sculpture centered slightly right. Overlay oversized cold-white condensed sans-serif typography across the foreground, stacked and intentionally cropped: exact words "NEW / ART / TEST / 2026". Several foreground letters must be extremely close to camera and heavily defocused while central letters remain partly sharp, creating a layered depth-of-field veil across the sculpture. Add thin white elliptical orbit lines curving behind and in front of the figure.

At upper left place the exact date "15—20 JULY" and beneath it small clean editorial lines reading "RESOURCE / HOLOGRAPHIC FORM / GRAPHIC POSTER STUDY". At upper right add a small plain "genvizu" wordmark inside a simple outlined rectangle, not a logo imitation.

Add three small left-aligned text blocks using the exact headings "FORM", "LIGHT", and "MATERIAL" with short readable neutral descriptions; add one small paragraph near lower left. Typography is crisp Swiss-style editorial except for the intentionally blurred giant foreground letters.

Palette black, porcelain white, silver, and restrained antique gold. No third-party audio logo, no real website, no lorem ipsum, no watermark, no signature, no QR code, no exposed anatomy, no religious iconography. Avoid extra figures, rainbow color, flat lighting, plastic toy look, malformed hands, perfectly centered symmetry.
