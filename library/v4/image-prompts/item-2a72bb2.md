Create a 3:4 vertical, intimate fine-art photographic portrait of a clearly adult woman, age 25, framed as a very close upper-torso bust portrait. She has a soft oval face, a narrow gently rounded jaw, a subtly pointed chin, smooth natural light skin with a warm-neutral undertone, a slim straight nose, relaxed closed eyelids, softly shaped brows, and full coral-pink lips resting slightly parted. Her deep brown hair is gathered into a loose, messy updo; heavy irregular wispy bangs fall across most of her forehead and partly veil one eye area, while delicate curled flyaways trace her temple, cheek, and ear. Her head is lowered and tilted slightly to one side, her expression peaceful, inward, and quietly vulnerable.

FIDELITY — HIGHEST PRIORITY: Reconstruct the written facial relationships, closed-eye expression, deep-brown tousled updo and broken fringe, warm-neutral skin tone, slender natural shoulder-and-neck proportions, exact ivory-white layered garment construction, lowered head angle, close upper-torso crop, directional sunlight, dark plain interior, restrained warm palette, and hushed private atmosphere. Her long neck, collarbones, and natural shoulder line are visible. Two narrow white shoulder straps remain clearly in place. She wears an opaque ivory-white cotton blouse or dress with abundant soft gathers and broad ruffled fabric slipping loosely from the outer shoulders and upper arms; the opaque gathered bodice securely and completely covers the chest. Large tactile folds cascade across the lower half of the frame. Her forearms draw gently inward beneath the gathered fabric near the bottom crop, with no provocative gesture and no intimate exposure. Preserve realistic fabric weight, compression, overlap, and contact shadows.

Use a near-frontal close camera position with a subtle downward relationship, natural portrait perspective, and a crop from just above the hair crown to the upper torso. Let the subject fill nearly the entire frame. A warm, narrow beam of late-afternoon or early-morning sunlight enters from the upper right, crossing the bangs, nose, cheek, neck, collarbones, and white folds, while the opposite side falls into deep soft shadow. Keep the background a nearly featureless charcoal-brown interior with shallow depth of field. Use low saturation, creamy beige highlights, gentle halation, soft focus falloff, delicate film grain, and a painterly photographic finish.

The adult attraction is conveyed only through camera closeness, the graceful covered shoulder-and-collarbone line, the tactile volume of opaque ruffled cotton, the lowered head, closed eyes, and warm light shaping the face and fabric. The moment should feel like a quiet private pause, tender and self-contained, never performative. Anatomically natural proportions, authentic skin texture, coherent hands and arms, clean fabric edges, no explicit nudity, no intimate exposure, no transparent clothing.
