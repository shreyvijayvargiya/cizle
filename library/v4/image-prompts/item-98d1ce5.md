```
Create a photorealistic 2×2 character-editorial collage in an approximately 3:4 vertical format. Every panel must feature the same clearly adult woman, visually around 22 to 28 years old, with consistent facial structure, makeup, hair color, maid costume and headwear. Do not merge the four images into one continuous scene.

She has a compact oval face, a narrow jaw and prominently doll-like eye proportions. Her makeup combines Japanese gothic-lolita portraiture, doll-inspired editorials and Korean social-media beauty styling: a pale semi-matte base, fine natural brows, gray-green or cool-gray enlarging contacts, elongated black eyeliner, clearly separated upper and lower lashes, soft pink shading beneath the eyes, a few tiny freckle-like or beauty-mark dots, a gentle nose-tip highlight and muted gray-pink nude lips. Her lips remain slightly parted without a smile. In the upper-left close-up, she opens her eyes wide and looks directly into the lens. In the other panels, her eyelids are more relaxed or her gaze shifts slightly sideways. The overall expression should feel quiet, detached and self-possessed, like an ornate doll that has developed a will of its own.

Her hair is deep chestnut brown, reflecting warm chocolate-brown satin highlights under direct flash. It is long enough to reach the chest or waist and paired with a neat, softly airy blunt fringe. Preserve two curved face-framing sections, slightly inward-bending ends and loose natural arcs. Include a few flyaway hairs, the natural spread of hair in the reclining panel and small variations in fringe placement between photographs. Avoid a rigid wig texture or perfectly uniform styling.

She wears a vintage Japanese-inspired maid dress in deep navy and creamy white. The bodice has a square neckline, a white apron panel, short navy puff sleeves, white eyelet-lace cuffs and dark satin bow details. The skirt is short and flared, with a layered white ruffled apron and lace petticoat. The lace appears light, thin and mildly translucent, with fine visible texture. The navy fabric is thicker and holds a structured bell-shaped silhouette. Add smoky-black sheer thigh-high stockings and glossy black platform Mary Jane heels. Her wide black-and-white maid headband is built from sculptural creamy ruffles, dark satin ribbons and large black bows on both sides.

Visible skin includes the full face, neck, a small amount of collarbone, upper arms, forearms, hands and small areas of upper-thigh skin between the skirt and stockings. Her complexion is light neutral ivory with a subtle cool-pink undertone. Hard flash makes the skin appear creamy and bright, while shadowed areas shift toward soft gray-beige. The surface looks carefully made-up but still visually suggests a soft, fine, slightly cool and faintly powdery touch. Preserve natural joint color variation, subtle pores and small creases around the fingers, knees and arms. Avoid plastic or silicone-like skin.

The upper-left panel is a close bust portrait photographed near eye level. She looks directly into the camera and lightly touches the area beneath one eye or the cheek with a finger. Part of a black handgun-shaped theatrical prop enters from the side of the frame. The composition is tight, with the maid headpiece and hair filling the upper section and the square neckline and apron panel visible below.

The upper-right panel is a full-body pose seated in an ornate classical armchair. Her legs bend and overlap across the seat and foreground, clearly showing the sheer black stockings and platform heels. Both arms rise above her head, with a black handgun-shaped character prop in each hand. The arms form an imperfect triangular frame. The navy skirt and white lace spread outward, revealing rich layers of ruffles.

The lower-left panel is photographed directly from above. She lies on a brown floor with her head near the bottom edge of the panel. Her long hair spreads naturally like a fan. Do not rotate or correct this composition. Both hands hold a black handgun-shaped prop near the chest. The navy skirt and white lace gather above the body, while one bent leg enters the frame. Preserve a slight imbalance and spontaneous snapshot feeling.

The lower-right panel uses a three-quarter side view. She leans forward over a dark upright piano. One hand braces near the piano keys or casing, while the other lowers a black prop toward the floor. Her head turns back toward the camera. Long hair falls beside the face and along the back. The navy skirt forms a bell-shaped silhouette behind her, with the white lace hem remaining clearly visible.

All handgun-shaped objects must read as non-functional theatrical or cosplay props used only for fashion and suspense storytelling. Do not show real model markings, internal mechanics, firing procedures, muzzle flashes, shell casings, blood or injury. Do not point the props toward the viewer.

The setting is a dim and elaborate vintage music room, Victorian-inspired parlor or gothic private salon. Include a dark upright piano with visible keys, a carved wooden armchair with pale gray patterned upholstery, a vintage lamp with fringe or beaded trim, layered gray-brown and bronze satin drapes, a round carved side table, glasses, small bottles, a gold tray and a framed vintage butterfly-specimen display. The room should resemble a time-frozen antique dollhouse: refined, enclosed, quiet and faintly uncanny.

Photograph the collage like an early-2000s compact digital camera or early social-media character editorial. Use hard direct flash close to the lens axis. The face, white apron and lace become bright, while concentrated highlights appear across the nose bridge, under-eye area, backs of the hands, arms and thighs. Short, clearly edged shadows form beneath the fringe, chin, lace trim and skirt layers. The light falls off rapidly, pushing the piano, curtains, dark wood furniture and corners of the room into deep brown or black underexposure. Retain only weak warm tungsten light and bronze-gold environmental reflections. Do not add blue rim lighting, large softboxes or evenly balanced commercial fill.

Preserve slight highlight bloom, dark-area noise, JPEG compression, uneven local sharpness, exposure variation and small white-balance differences between panels. The poses, stacked skirt layers, flyaway hairs and cropping do not need to be perfectly polished. She had already changed into the maid costume and entered the dim old music room. She now touches her cheek, raises both hands, lies beneath the camera and finally leans toward the piano. The next panel feels as though it might reveal the story hidden beneath the sweet styling. Blend Japanese gothic maid fashion, dark lolita, doll-like makeup, Victorian interiors and early digital direct-flash photography.
```
