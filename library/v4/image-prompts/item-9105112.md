Distinctive composition following Armin Hofmann grid-based precision, incorporating Kenyan Hara minimalist space-negation and Malika Favre inspired abstract vector silhouettes. Lighting and atmosphere
High-contrast vibrant color palette with bold complementary hues. The lighting is flat and uniform, emphasizing the sleek, uncluttered, and upscale atmosphere. A dreamlike, serene vibe pervades the scene, characterized by a refined, tender narrative charm.

Material and texture
Crisp clean lines and bold linearity characteristic of high-end graphic design. The aesthetic combines large solid-color blocks with a sophisticated flat paint texture. Subtle, expressive brushwork elements are layered with a refined, premium fine art finish, achieving an ultra-high-definition visual clarity.

Constraints
No text, no watermarks, no distorted anatomy, no disjointed elements, no messy shadows, no plastic-looking surfaces.
