Create an ultra-photorealistic cinematic portrait in a 3:4 vertical aspect ratio. A stunning adult East Asian woman in her late 20s is kneeling or crouching beside the edge of a bed, photographed from a very steep overhead angle. Reproduce the reference composition precisely: the camera looks almost vertically downward; her upturned face occupies the central upper portion of the frame; her shoulders, upper torso, bent thighs, and part of her kneeling posture remain visible below. The strong overhead foreshortening naturally emphasizes her face, shoulders, and curvy upper body.

She has a mature, naturally voluptuous hourglass figure: full rounded bust, soft broad shoulders, a narrow waist, rounded hips, and thick shapely thighs visible near the lower edges of the frame. Her proportions must look physically believable, weight-bearing, and naturally affected by the crouched posture. The body should have realistic softness, skin compression, collarbone structure, shoulder folds, and subtle perspiration rather than an artificially perfect model shape.

Her face is exceptionally beautiful with refined East Asian features: a softly tapered oval face, delicate jawline, large dark-brown almond-shaped eyes, a straight petite nose, softly rounded cheeks, and naturally full rose-pink lips. Preserve realistic pores, tiny facial hairs, faint under-eye texture, natural asymmetry, uneven moisture, and subtle redness around the nose and eyelids. She must look like a real photographed person, not a beauty-filtered influencer or digital character.

Her gaze and emotional state are the main focus. She tilts her chin upward while looking vaguely toward the camera, but her eyes are not sharply focused on the lens. Her pupils sit slightly high beneath lowered upper eyelids; her lower eyelids are swollen with moisture; her gaze drifts just past the camera as if she is emotionally exhausted and struggling to stay fully present. Her eyes are glossy, unfocused, hazy, and dreamlike, with small trembling catchlights reflected in the wet tear film. The two eyes should remain naturally aligned, without crossed eyes.

Her inner eyebrows rise and pull gently together, creating a restrained, vulnerable expression. Her cheeks are intensely flushed. Her lips are damp and softly parted, revealing only a narrow glimpse of her upper teeth. The lower lip hangs slightly loose, suggesting shallow breathing and emotional fatigue. Her expression combines confusion, longing, exhaustion, and fragile vulnerability. She is not openly sobbing, smiling, frightened, or posing seductively; she appears quietly overwhelmed, dazed, and emotionally spent.

A thin layer of perspiration covers her forehead, temples, cheeks, nose, neck, collarbones, shoulders, and upper chest. Tiny droplets form irregularly along the hairline and jaw. Transparent tears pool along both lower eyelids, with one or two fine tear trails running naturally down the cheeks. Keep the moisture physically realistic: varied droplet sizes, broken highlights, damp pores, and no uniform artificial body-oil shine.

Her black hair is gathered into a loose, partially undone low updo. Damp wispy strands cling across her forehead, temples, cheeks, and the bridge of her nose. Several strands cross the face and one passes close to an eye, but both eyes remain clearly readable. Fine flyaway hairs catch the warm side light. She wears a single ornate antique-gold floral earring with a delicate dangling pearl chain.

She wears a fully opaque black off-the-shoulder period-inspired dress with a wide white gathered lace ruffle tracing the neckline. The fitted black bodice supports and securely covers her full bust, with realistic fabric tension, seams, folds, and compression created by her crouched posture. Add ornate antique-gold baroque embroidery along the upper sleeves and shoulder edges. The neckline, costume structure, shoulders, and upper-body silhouette should match the reference without nudity or wardrobe malfunction.

A second adult person’s hand enters diagonally from the lower foreground and gently cups the side of her face. The heel of the palm supports her jaw while the fingers rest softly behind her ear and along the cheek. Her cheek presses naturally into the palm with subtle skin displacement. The gesture is protective and consensual, not forceful. The forearm remains slightly blurred in the foreground while her wet eyes stay in precise focus.

Use an intimate top-down camera position approximately 65–75 degrees above her eye line, close enough to create emotional proximity but without fisheye distortion. Use a full-frame camera with a 50mm lens at f/1.8. Focus exactly on the irises and wet lower eyelashes; allow the ears, shoulders, hand, dress, thighs, wooden floor, and unmade pale bed linens to fall gradually into soft blur.

Warm dim amber bedside light comes from one side, producing soft sculpted shadows beneath the eyelashes, nose, lips, chin, and collarbones. Add very gentle neutral fill light so both eyes remain visible. Deep brown, black, ivory, and antique-gold color palette, restrained cinematic contrast, natural highlight roll-off, subtle high-ISO film grain, realistic dynamic range, unretouched editorial photography, emotionally intense arthouse film still.
