FACE IDENTITY IS THE HIGHEST PRIORITY. She has a softly narrow oval adult doll-face, gentle cheek volume, subtle apple cheeks, a smooth tapered jaw, a small rounded chin, dark almond eyes looking directly at the camera, naturally fine dark brows, a slim low-to-medium nose bridge, a small rounded nose tip, and muted-pink lips opened in a warm modest tooth-showing smile. COMPOSITION ORIENTATION — DO NOT MIRROR: The tattooed resting arm must remain on viewer-left. The raised hand and red-white bubble device must occupy the upper-right quadrant of the image. The device must never appear on viewer-left. The smiling face sits left of the raised device. The second woven-cane chair back overlaps the lower-right foreground. Over it she wears faded pale blue-gray oversized denim overalls. The roomy trousers create heavy realistic folds around both raised knees and crossed lower legs. Preserve natural adult proportions and the faithful covered chest-to-waist contour—neither enlarged nor flattened. The fitted lined camisole, low scoop construction, seated forward-diagonal ribcage angle, gravity, lace tension, slightly high camera, and soft contact shadows make the covered contour readable while secure coverage stays intact. Place the woman slightly left of center. Preserve the second cane chair back overlapping the lower-right foreground, pale floor and wall, bright upper-left window, and softly blurred wooden shelf with green plants at far right.

The scene feels like an interrupted adult afternoon at home: cheerful, intimate, playful, pure-yet-sensual, and naturally unposed. Render realistic hands, feet, chair joinery, denim folds, lace edges, bubble reflections, gravity, and contact shadows. Remove all third-party marks, character designs, logos, text, watermarks, signatures, account IDs, QR codes, and private information. No explicit nudity, transparent intimate fabric, intimate exposure, or anatomy-centered composition.
