Core Concept:
Redraw Homelander as a character from an American adult animated sitcom (similar to Family Guy, The Simpsons, or Rick and Morty).

Key Features to Retain:

Iconic Silhouette: Keep the specific hairstyle, signature color palette, clothing outline, and key accessories (e.g., Jotaro’s hat-hair blend or Giorno’s chest cutout).

Personality: Maintain the character's original "vibe" or temperament (e.g., stoic, flamboyant, or rebellious).

Visual Language / Art Style:

Line Work: Bold, thick black outlines.

Eyes: Large white circular eyes with small black pupils (classic "googly" or "dot" eye look).

Features: Highly simplified facial features and anatomy.

Proportions: "Big head, simple body" aesthetic (exaggerated cartoon proportions).

Coloring: Flat cel-shading with no complex gradients or textures.

Background: A clean, simplified indoor setting (e.g., a living room or a kitchen).
