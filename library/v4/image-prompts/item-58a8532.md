Photographic poetic realism, soft dreamlike Japanese film photography, inspired by Rinko Kawauchi, gentle overexposure, diffused morning light, pastel color palette, airy softness, subtle grain, quiet intimacy, poetic everyday life, fragile emotional atmosphere, vertical 2:3 composition.

A young adult Japanese female idol in her early 20s, delicate and natural beauty, soft wispy bangs, slightly messy dark hair loosely tied back, luminous fair skin with natural texture, minimal makeup, a quiet and innocent presence, fresh and unforced.

Scene: inside a quiet Tokyo apartment in the early morning. Soft daylight passes through thin white curtains, filling the room with pale glowing light. The room feels less like a set and more like a memory.

Action: she is gently biting into a red apple, captured in a candid in-between moment. The apple becomes the only vivid color accent in the soft pale frame. The action feels small, ordinary, and deeply alive.

Pose: natural and unposed, sitting loosely by the window, on the floor beside the bed, or at a small table. Her body is relaxed, slightly leaning, one hand holding the apple, the other hand resting absent-mindedly on her lap, on the floor, or lightly touching her clothing. No dramatic pose, no performance.

Expression: calm, distant, absent-minded, half-awake, as if quietly listening to the morning. Neutral emotion, soft gaze drifting toward the window light rather than the camera.

Lighting: extremely soft diffused natural light, slightly hazy, with lifted shadows and gently blown highlights. No hard edges, no flash, no strong contrast. Skin appears milky, luminous, and softly overexposed.

Color tone: warm white, pale cream, soft beige, washed gray-blue, faint greenish shadows, muted skin tones, light pastel atmosphere, no harsh blacks.

Image feel: 35mm Japanese film photography, soft focus edges, delicate grain, shallow but natural depth of field, floating highlights, visual softness like breath on glass. The image should feel intimate, fleeting, and almost disappearing.

Overall mood: everyday life turned into poetry, quiet loneliness mixed with comfort, gentle morning stillness, a soft fragment of memory rather than a styled portrait.
