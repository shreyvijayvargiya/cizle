Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic surreal-fashion portrait of a clearly adult East Asian woman, age 23, floating sideways through a vast pink-lilac cloudscape. She has a refined oval face, balanced cheek volume, straight dark brows, almond-shaped dark eyes gazing softly upward, a natural straight nose, muted rose lips, warm light-beige skin with authentic texture, and long black-brown hair streaming upward in separate weightless strands. She wears a dusty-rose couture gown with a securely lined opaque strapless folded bodice, fine horizontal micro-pleating, a fitted natural waist, and an enormous layered chiffon-and-silk skirt.

CORRECTIVE FIDELITY — HIGHEST PRIORITY: Keep the successful mature East Asian appearance, rose couture pleating, dramatic flowing skirt, coral sunset, volumetric clouds, realistic fabric, and cinematic light. Correct only the body orientation and pose geometry. Rotate her entire figure approximately 90 degrees relative to the vertical canvas: her head is on the right side of the frame, her skirt and legs extend toward the left, and the line through shoulders, waist, and hips is nearly horizontal across the middle third. She is reclining in midair, not standing, climbing, leaping, flying upward, or posed vertically.

Her torso remains sideways and almost level, the right shoulder slightly lower, one knee bent toward the upper-left under opaque drapery, and the opposite leg extended toward the left beneath layered fabric. Her right arm drifts downward toward the lower-right with an open relaxed hand; her left arm trails behind along the horizontal motion. Make the securely covered waist-to-hip-to-leg transition the primary line through the fitted pleated waist, sideways weight, controlled knee bend, realistic fabric tension, and low three-quarter perspective—neither enlarged nor flattened, never anatomy-centered. Arrange the skirt as three rose-pink petal fins flowing leftward, with translucent outer edges but fully opaque body coverage. Leave generous pink cloud space above and below the horizontal figure. Use a 50 mm lens from slightly beneath her, full-body crop, warm coral sunrise from upper right, cool mauve fill from below, gentle volumetric haze, pastel film color, fine grain, and restrained highlight bloom. Mature weightless calm and quiet wonder. No text, logo, watermark, signature, QR code, explicit nudity, or intimate exposure.
