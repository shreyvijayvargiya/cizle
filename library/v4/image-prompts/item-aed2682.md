prompt⬇️
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical futuristic editorial campaign poster, 4:5 aspect ratio

Create a high-fashion, minimalist speculative-technology portrait poster. A clearly adult Black man is shown from behind in a close three-quarter side profile, facing right. His close-cropped textured hair, head, long neck, high technical collar and broad shoulders form one monumental near-black silhouette rising from the lower-left and occupying roughly two-thirds of the frame. Crop the left shoulder and both lower shoulders at the canvas edges. Leave a large field of clean cold-white negative space above the head and across the entire right side. Preserve the deliberately left-heavy, asymmetrical balance.

The visual focus is a single oversized wraparound futuristic visor covering the eye area from temple to temple: a broad smoked-black translucent lens with an elegant continuous aerodynamic curve, a subtle inward wave around the nose bridge, and a razor-thin warm orange-red illuminated rim. The visor should feel engineered, tactile and premium, not like ordinary sunglasses. Add one tiny horizontal orange highlight near the upper lens. Stretch a long horizontal motion smear and soft scanning blur from the visor toward the left edge, with layered charcoal streaks fading into the white field, suggesting speed, signal and temporal movement. Keep the right lens edge and orange rim crisp while the left side dissolves into motion.

Render the face and neck almost entirely in deep matte black, without readable facial identity, visible eyes or bright skin; retain only minimal cool-gray contour information at the cheek, ear hardware, jaw, neck and collar. Show realistic microtexture in the close-cropped hair, subtle skin grain, dense black technical fabric and a small sculptural black side hinge behind the visor. The clothing is a severe black high-neck performance garment with no seams competing for attention.

Lighting is extreme high-key white backlight with a soft luminous halo around the head and shoulders, deep crushed blacks, restrained cool-gray edge separation and slight bloom. Use fine analog film grain, halftone-like print noise, subtle optical haze and faint texture in the white background. The finish should combine experimental fashion photography, premium editorial graphic design and late-2020s wearable-tech art direction. Strict palette: cold white, carbon black, tiny amounts of cool gray, and one controlled orange-red luminous accent. No colorful cyberpunk environment, no city, no extra props, no blue neon, no glossy plastic skin.

Place a compact white typography block on the dark neck area, around the lower-left quadrant, with generous surrounding black space. Use a clean modern geometric sans-serif, left aligned. Set the headline in bold uppercase on two lines, verbatim:
“SIGNAL CLEAR.”
“FOCUS FORWARD.”

Below it, set smaller regular-weight copy with tight leading, verbatim:
“Cut through the noise.”
“Wear the future.”
“No doubt. Just intent.”
“One breath. One step. Forward.”

At the very bottom center, add a tiny two-line white brand lockup, verbatim:
“genvizu”
“BUILT FOR FOCUS.”

All text must be sharply legible and correctly spelled. Do not include any other logo, watermark, signature, social handle, user ID, QR code, copyright line or third-party brand. Preserve the stark negative space, severe silhouette, horizontal visor blur, grainy tactile finish and quiet technological confidence.
