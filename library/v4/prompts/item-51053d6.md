# 手工艺传承记

Community prompt · 纪录片
Language: zh · Domains: entertainment, nature · Source: K03

Source page: https://github.com/c1596626866-sudo/directors-vision-bible

```
微距镜头记录一位老木匠手工制作榫卯结构的过程, 100mm微距近距离拍摄粗糙的双手和木料, 刨子在木料表面推过卷起薄如蝉翼的木花, 凿子在木头上敲击时木屑飞溅的慢镜瞬间, 木料的年轮纹理在侧光下清晰可见如同山水画, 手指在榫头表面反复摩挲确认平整度, 阳光从老作坊的高窗斜射下来在空气中形成光柱, 工具在角落整齐排列泛着岁月的光泽, 木屑和刨花散落在布满痕迹的工作台上, 暖棕色调, 安静而有力量的工匠纪录画面
```

Macro lens documenting an elderly woodworker handcrafting traditional mortise and tenon joints, 100mm macro tight on the weathered hands and wood, a plane gliding over wood surface peeling off tissue-thin wood shavings, slow motion moment of wood chips flying as a chisel strikes the timber, the annual ring textures of the wood clearly visible in side light resembling Chinese ink landscape painting, fingers repeatedly running over the tenon surface checking for perfect flatness, sunlight slanting down from the high window of the old workshop forming light shafts in the dusty air, tools neatly arranged in the corner gleaming with the patina of age, sawdust and wood shavings scattered across the time-worn workbench, warm brown tones, quiet yet powerful craftsman documentary frame
