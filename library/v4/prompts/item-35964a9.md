# 雨夜独处情绪

Community prompt · 情绪 Vlog
Language: zh · Domains: general · Source: K03

Source page: https://github.com/c1596626866-sudo/directors-vision-bible

```
雨夜的一间温暖公寓, 35mm镜头从室内看向窗户, 雨水在玻璃上形成一道道细流模糊了窗外城市的霓虹灯光使其变成流动的粉蓝色光斑, 室内只有一盏暖黄的落地灯照亮了一个角落, 沙发上盖着毛毯的一角有一个人蜷缩着只露出半个背影和手中的书页, 一杯热红茶在木茶几上冒着几乎看不见的白气, 旁边一只灰猫蜷成完美的圆安静地睡着, 墙上黑胶唱片机缓慢旋转播放着爵士乐, 暖黄和冷蓝的色调对比, 清冷而温暖的独处时光
```

English version:

```
Rainy night in a warm apartment, 35mm lens looking from inside toward the window, rainwater forming rivulets down the glass blurring the city's neon lights outside into flowing pink and blue bokeh, only a warm yellow floor lamp illuminating one corner of the room, a person curled up on the sofa under a blanket with only half their back and the pages of a book visible, a cup of hot black tea barely steaming on the wooden coffee table, a grey cat beside it curled into a perfect circle sleeping peacefully, a vinyl record player on the wall slowly spinning jazz, warm yellow and cool blue color contrast, a moment of solitary comfort that's both cool and warm
```
