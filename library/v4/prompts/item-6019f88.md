# 书店安静时光

Community prompt · 情绪 Vlog
Language: zh · Domains: general · Source: K03

Source page: https://github.com/c1596626866-sudo/directors-vision-bible

```
一个老书店的安静午后, 35mm镜头缓慢推轨穿过两侧高耸至天花板的木质书架形成的深邃走廊, 阳光从高处的窗户投射下来在过道中形成一道道暖色的光带尘埃在光中缓慢飘浮, 走廊尽头一位老人坐在皮质扶手椅上戴着老花镜专注地阅读膝上的精装书, 手指缓慢翻过泛黄的书页在特写中纸纤维的纹理清晰可见, 书店的猫蜷缩在一摞旧书上面睡得正香, 木质书架散发出年代的气息, 暖木色和纸米白的沉浸色调, 安静的知识时光
```

English version:

```
Quiet afternoon in an old bookstore, 35mm lens slowly tracking through a deep corridor formed by towering wooden bookshelves reaching the ceiling on both sides, sunlight streaming down from high windows casting warm bands of light in the aisle with dust motes slowly floating in the beams, at the far end of the corridor an elderly person seated in a leather armchair wearing reading glasses deeply absorbed in a hardcover book on their lap, fingers slowly turning yellowed pages in close-up the texture of paper fibers clearly visible, the bookstore cat curled up fast asleep on a stack of old books, the wooden shelves exuding the scent of age, warm wood tones and paper cream white immersive color palette, quiet time with knowledge
```
