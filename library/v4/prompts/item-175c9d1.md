# 人物肖像访谈

Community prompt · 纪录片
Language: zh · Domains: entertainment, nature · Source: K03

Source page: https://github.com/c1596626866-sudo/directors-vision-bible

```
一位老手艺人坐在自己工作了几十年的作坊窗前, 85mm镜头拍摄中近景, 侧面45度的窗光是唯一的光源, 柔和的天光勾勒出他布满皱纹的面部轮廓, 眼睛中反射着窗户的光斑成为画面的灵魂所在, 银白色的头发在逆光中微微发光, 粗糙的双手安静地放在膝盖上, 虚化的背景中隐约可见老旧的工具和工作台的轮廓, 故事写在每一道皱纹里, 自然暖色调, 浅景深的纪录片人物肖像
```

English version:

```
An elderly craftsman sitting by the window of the workshop where he has worked for decades, 85mm lens medium close-up, side 45-degree window light as the only light source, soft daylight sculpting the contours of his deeply wrinkled face, the reflection of the window in his eyes becoming the soul of the frame, silver-white hair faintly glowing in the backlight, rough hands resting quietly on his lap, old tools and workbench vaguely visible in the defocused background, a lifetime of stories written in every wrinkle, natural warm tones, shallow depth of field documentary portrait
```
